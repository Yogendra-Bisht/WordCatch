# Privacy Policy for WordCatch

**Last updated:** September 2026

WordCatch is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, used, and safeguarded when using the WordCatch extension.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Details:** Email address used to sign up and authenticate your account.
- **Saved Words & Definitions:** Vocabulary words you manually save, along with definitions, phonetics, and parts of speech.
- **Source Page URL:** The web address of the page where a word was saved, allowing you to recall context.
- **Timestamps:** Dates when words were saved to organize your personal dictionary.

## 2. Information We DO NOT Collect
- Browsing history or page visiting logs.
- Personal data or text typed on web pages.
- Data sharing or selling to third-party ad networks.

## 3. Storage and Security
- Password credentials are stored as salted `bcrypt` hashes.
- Communication between extension and backend occurs strictly over encrypted HTTPS (`https://wordcatch.onrender.com`).
- Database records are stored securely in cloud-hosted MongoDB Atlas clusters.

## 4. Contact
For support or inquiries regarding privacy, please visit our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Yogendra-Bisht/WordCatch).
