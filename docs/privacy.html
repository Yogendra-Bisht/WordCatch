WordCatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

WordCatch only collects data essential for operating your personal vocabulary builder account:

  • Email Address: Used strictly for account creation, login authentication, and account recovery.
  • Saved Words & Definitions: Words you choose to save, along with their definitions and phonetics.
  • Source Web Page URL: The URL of the webpage where you saved the word, allowing you to revisit the context later.
  • Timestamps: The date and time when a word was saved to organize your vocabulary by date.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not track or monitor your browsing history.
  • We do not log web pages you visit unless you explicitly double-click and save a word.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking pixels inside the extension.

3. Where & How Data Is Stored

Your data is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our backend API hosted on Render and stored in a secure MongoDB Atlas cloud database cluster.

4. Authentication & Security

Passwords are securely hashed using bcrypt before being stored. We never store plain-text passwords.

5. Third-Party Services

WordCatch fetches word definitions using the public Free Dictionary API. No user credentials or personal identifiers are sent to external dictionary providers.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can view, edit, or delete any saved words from your account at any time through the extension interface. You may also request account deletion by contacting us.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub repository support at GitHub repository.
